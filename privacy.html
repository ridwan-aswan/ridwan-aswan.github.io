Privacy Policy — Fakta Unik Uploader

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Who uses this app

Fakta Unik Uploader is a private, personal tool. Its only user is the app owner, who connects their own

TikTok account to upload their own videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts.

2. Data we access

After the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the basic account identifier

(open_id) for the owner's account, with the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload.

No data from any other TikTok user is accessed or collected.

3. How data is used

The access token is used only to upload the owner's own video files to the owner's TikTok inbox

through the official Content Posting API. Nothing is published automatically.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file that is not shared, sold or sent

to any third party. No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

5. Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the local token file at any time and can revoke the app's access in TikTok

settings (Security and permissions > Apps and services).

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from children.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the latest version is published at this address.

Questions can be sent to the app owner through the TikTok account that uses this app.
